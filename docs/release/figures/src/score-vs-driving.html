A score is not a verdict: the same six models, ranked by two benchmarks
Predicting what a driver did and driving safely are different skills, and each benchmark rewards one of them.
Qwen-Drive: 1st → 5th
Best at predicting what the driver did; near the bottom once it has to drive through a hazard.
openpilot: 6th → 3rd
Last open loop, in the top group closed loop, and the only model here that already drives in customers' cars.
Constant velocity: 2nd → 6th
"Keep going as you are" is second open loop and last when something is in the way.
So no single number decides
Paddock reports open and closed loop side by side, pairs every comparison sample by sample, flags runs that are not comparable, and shows the failure behind a score.
